FAQ

Good
questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on Instagram.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes, once it's set up. You need a connection one time to install the app and download the model. After that, the model runs on your phone and answers questions with no Wi-Fi or cell signal. You only need to go online again for app and knowledge updates.

Which phones will it run on?

SurvivedAI is coming to iPhone and Android. Running a language model locally needs a reasonably recent phone and some free storage for the model. We'll publish exact device and storage requirements before launch.

Will it drain my battery?

Generating an answer uses your phone's processor, so it uses more power than reading a text, but only while it's answering. When it isn't working on a question, it isn't using the model. In a long outage or on a multi-day trip, carry a charged power bank and keep your screen brightness down.

Is it medical advice?

No. SurvivedAI provides general information to help you think clearly and prepare. It is not medical advice and it is not a substitute for a doctor or for emergency services. AI can make mistakes, so check critical information. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Is it free?

The base model is free. A Premium plan with a smarter, survival-trained model and monthly reviewed knowledge updates is planned at $9.99 per year. Premium is not available yet.

Where do my chats go?

They stay on your phone. The assistant answers on the device, so your questions don't have to be sent to a server to get a reply. The free version doesn't need an account.

When is it launching?

Soon. SurvivedAI is in prelaunch and coming to iPhone and Android. The best way to hear the date first is to follow us on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube.
