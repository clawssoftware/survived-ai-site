FAQ

Straight answers.

Does it really work offline?

Yes. Download the AI model once. After that it answers with no Wi-Fi or signal.

Is it free?

Free to start. Premium features are planned after launch.

Which phones?

iPhone and Android. We'll publish exact requirements before launch.

Where do my chats go?

Nowhere. They stay on your phone.

Is it medical advice?

No. It gives general information and can make mistakes. In an emergency, call 911.
