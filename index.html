How it works

Airplane mode on.
Answers still coming.

  1. 01

    Download once

    While you have Wi-Fi or data, install the app and download the model to your phone. It's a one-time download.

  2. 02

    Lose the signal

    Airplane mode, a dead zone, a downed tower. The model is already on your phone, so it doesn't need a connection.

  3. 03

    Ask anything

    Type your question. The answer is generated right on the device, word by word.

Use cases

Built for the backcountry, and for the night the power goes out at home.

Out there

Hiking, camping, hunting, paddling, road trips through dead zones. For when there's nobody to call and no bars to call with.

  • How do I make stream water safe to drink?
  • What are the early signs of hypothermia?
  • A storm is moving in above treeline. What now?
  • How do I signal for help without a phone signal?
  • I think I'm off the trail. How do I get my bearings?

At home

Outages, floods, heat waves, cold snaps, boil-water notices, evacuations. The moments when the network is the first thing to go.

  • The power's been out six hours. Is the fridge food still safe?
  • The AC is out in a heat wave. How do we stay cool?
  • How do I run a generator safely?
  • What goes in an evacuation bag?
  • The pipes might freeze tonight. What should I do?

Example questions. SurvivedAI gives general information, and AI can make mistakes, so check critical information.

Private by design

The model lives on your phone. So do your conversations. Nothing has to leave the device to get an answer.

  • Runs on the device

    Answers come from a model stored on your phone. No server round-trip, so it works with zero bars.

  • Chats stay local

    Your conversation history is saved on your phone. Your questions don't need to be sent anywhere to be answered.

  • No account needed

    The free version works without signing up. Open the app, download the model, start asking.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Something else on your mind? Ask us on Instagram.

Does it really work without internet?

Yes, once it's set up. You need a connection one time to install the app and download the model. After that, the model runs on your phone and answers with no Wi-Fi or cell signal. You only need to go online again for updates.

Is it free?

Free to start. Premium features are planned after launch.

Which phones will it run on?

SurvivedAI is coming to iPhone and Android. Running a language model locally needs a reasonably recent phone and some free storage for the model. We'll publish exact requirements before launch.

Will it drain my battery?

Generating an answer uses your phone's processor, so it uses more power than reading a text, but only while it's answering. On a long outage or a multi-day trip, carry a charged power bank and keep your screen brightness down.

Is it medical advice?

No. SurvivedAI provides general information to help you think clearly and prepare. It is not medical advice and not a substitute for a doctor or emergency services. AI can make mistakes, so check critical information. In an emergency, call 911 or your local emergency number.

Where do my chats go?

They stay on your phone. The assistant answers on the device, so your questions don't have to be sent to a server to get a reply. The free version doesn't need an account.

When does it launch?

Soon. SurvivedAI is in prelaunch and coming to iPhone and Android. Follow us on Instagram, TikTok or YouTube to hear the date first.
